6.1(b) reduction modulo (p,g) is a ring homomorphism when g | f mod p
    By 6.1(a), mul(u,v)(t) = u(t)v(t) - q(t)f(t) with q integral. Reducing modulo p and g, and using g | f mod p,
    gives phi(uv) = phi(u)phi(v); phi is additive and phi(1) = 1. phi(l) = t is a root of f in F_p[t]/(g), since
    g | f; this is the image of l^3 - 4l + 6 = 0. phi is onto: every class mod g has a representative of degree
    < deg g <= 3. F_p[t]/(g) is a field because g is irreducible (Euclid's algorithm in F_p[t]). So P(p,g) =
    ker phi is closed under addition and under multiplication by R; and beta in P(p,g) gives (N(beta),0,0) =
    beta*adj(beta) in P(p,g), i.e. p | N(beta). Contrapositive: p does not divide N(pi) => pi not in P(p,g).
PASS  l^3 = 4l - 6 in R (the triple of l^3 - 4l + 6 is 0), matching f(t) = 0 in F_p[t]/(g) when g | f mod p
