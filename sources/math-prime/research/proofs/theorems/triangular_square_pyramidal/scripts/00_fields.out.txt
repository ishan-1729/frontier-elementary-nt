4. Substitution lemma: class form F_cl, field form G, and the bijection of their coprime solutions
====================================================================================================================
core.from_class(key, m, n) sends a solution (m,n) of F_cl to a solution (m',n') of G; core.to_class(key, m', n')
sends it back, or gives None when (m',n') comes from no solution of F_cl.  Right sides: class (1,0): (1, 4, 9, 36); class (0,1): (1, 4, 9, 36); class (1,1): (1, 4, 9, 36) (core.RHS = (1, 4, 9, 36)).
