# descent.classical: classical descents
Parent: descent     Children: none yet

These are the descents developed in [radical_descent_research](../../shared/prior/radical_descent_research/README.md):
- **Exact 2-adic valuation descent:** Fermat's square-area theorem, x⁴+y⁴=z², and the rational points on Y²=X³−X.
- **Auxiliary multiplier:** the two-square theorem for primes.
- **Denominator-prime count:** rational-to-integral representations by X²+DY², for D=1 and 2, and for D=3
  after a change of coordinates.
- **Weighted prime dependencies:** n²∣2ⁿ+1 ⇔ n∈{1,3} (proved in Lean), and related divisibility families.

The design principle stated there: simplify the quadratic form along the permitted directions of movement, then
descend along them while keeping the original constraints.
