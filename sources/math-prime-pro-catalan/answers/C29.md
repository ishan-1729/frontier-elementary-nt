# C29: A finite one-element Thaine reduction and its prime gap
written: 2026-10-29T01:21:00Z
question: C29: Thaine's step for one element, made finite — and which of its primes can be produced elementarily
status: partial — finite conditional derivative proved; simultaneous prime existence and source principalization open.

## 1. Scope and source assumptions
Let p<q be distinct odd primes, p∤(q−1)/2, F=Q(ζ_q), k=F⁺, L=F(ζ_p), H=(q−1)/2, G=Gal(k/Q). Assume the actual Catalan source hypotheses **SRC,CASS,DIV,DW,HALF,LEDGER,RAD** of C26–C28: X=−y, B_a=(X−ζ_q^a)(X−ζ_q^(−a)), α=B_g/B_1, the independent augmentation radical classes, p-divisible valuations and local pth roots. No global class-field theorem is assumed. The corrected parameter is c≡1(mod p), c≡p−1(mod q).

Let R=F_p[G], D the augmentation part of the reflected exponent Xi_c, and choose a nontrivial irreducible factor φ of T^H−1 on which D≠0. Let e_φ be its CRT projector, P_φ an integer lift, z=α^P_φ. By RAD, z is a non-pth-power strong-primary singular element. Write A=div(z)/p, an integral-coefficient fractional divisor, with p[A]=0.

## 2. Finite unit-component argument — PROVED under RAD
The real circular factors C_a=(1−ζ_q^a)(1−ζ_q^(−a)) have equal finite valuations, so C_g/C_1 is a totally positive unit. Put ε=(C_g/C_1)^P_φ.

For p-units define δ(v)=(v^p/σ_p(v)−1)/p modulo p, σ_p(ζ_q)=ζ_q^p. The finite binomial expansion from C28 gives δ(C_1)=(2/q)Σ_{a mod q} f(a)ζ_q^a, where f(a)=Σ_{r=1}^{p−1}r^(−1)[(a−r)p^(−1)]_q modulo p. The rational coefficient f(0) must be retained before applying T−1. The finite CRT identity D=(2/q)(1−σ_(p−1)^(−1))f implies e_φ(T−1)f≠0. Therefore δ(ε)≠0.

For completeness, the needed upper bound on the multiplicity of each nontrivial unit component can be proved without invoking Dirichlet's unit theorem. For any finitely generated G-stable subgroup V of positive units, the logarithm vectors log|σv| lie in the real augmentation hyperplane W of dimension H−1. They are discrete: if every coordinate of log(v) has magnitude <log(1+1/H), the integer trace of v lies in [H,H+1), hence equals H; AM–GM and norm(v)=1 force v=1. If rank(V)>dim(span log V), a box-pigeonhole argument on products of r generators with exponents 0,…,N gives (N+1)^r separated points in O(N)^d boxes, contradiction for large N. Thus V⊗R embeds G-equivariantly into W. Each nontrivial Hth-root eigenvalue occurs once in W, so the characteristic polynomial of G on V divides (T^H−1)/(T−1); reduction modulo p remains squarefree because p∤H. Taking finitely many representatives excludes two copies of the same simple factor in U/U^p. Since δ(ε)≠0 on φ, ε spans the φ-unit component and δ is injective there. In particular **every primary unit class on φ is zero**.

Consequently if div(β)=A for β∈k*, then z/β^p is a primary unit on φ and hence a pth power, contradicting RAD. Thus **[A]≠0 and p[A]=0**. This identifies the direct arithmetic target **PA_φ: explicitly principalize this one divided divisor**. It is not proved.

## 3. Finite Thaine derivative — PROVED conditionally on one prime
Let m=p^M annihilate the p-primary part of the finite divisor quotient of k. A crude elementary bound exists: with ξ=ζ_q+ζ_q^(−1), every integral ideal I of norm n contains a nonzero a=Σ_{i=0}^{H−1}b_i ξ^i with |b_i|≤⌈n^(1/H)⌉ and |N(a)|≤B_0 n, B_0=[2(2^H−1)]^H. Hence every class has an integral representative of norm ≤B_0. Hermite triangular matrices bound the number of index-≤B_0 sublattices by J=B_0^{H(H+1)/2}; take M minimal with p^M≥J. The requisite ideal arithmetic follows from integrality of O_k, the finite multiplication determinant, and the standard finite prime-ideal inverse construction (not a class-field theorem).

Suppose a prime ℓ≡1(mod qm), with λ|ℓ in k, satisfies [λ]=s[A] for 1≤s<p and ε^((ℓ−1)/p)≠1 mod λ. Write ε=∏ C_a^{E_a}, ΣE_a=0. Set n=ℓ−1, d=n/m, K=k(ζ_ℓ), choose a primitive residue r modulo ℓ, σ(ζ_ℓ)=ζ_ℓ^r, and define
U=∏_a(1−(ζ_q^a+ζ_q^(−a))ζ_ℓ+ζ_ℓ²)^{E_a}.
The finite geometric product gives N_(K/k)U=1 and U≡ε at λ. Put D_ℓ=Σ_{i=0}^{n−1}iσ^i, β=U^{D_ℓ}, V=U^d. Direct coefficient comparison yields (σ−1)D_ℓ=n−Σσ^i, hence σβ/β=V^m.

For a_i=∏_{j<i}σ^j(V), define ρ(t)=Σ_{i=0}^{n−1}a_i^(−1)σ^i(t). Reindexing proves σρ=Vρ. Some t among 1,ζ_ℓ,…,ζ_ℓ^{n−1} gives ρ≠0 by the Vandermonde determinant on the distinct conjugates of ζ_ℓ. Thus κ=β/ρ^m lies in k*. At λ the ramification index of K/k is n, and σ(ζ_ℓ−1)/(ζ_ℓ−1)≡r. Writing t=v_Λ(ρ) and e=log_r(ε mod λ), the eigen-equation gives t≡de(mod n), while n v_λ(κ)=−mt. Hence v_λ(κ)≡−e(mod m). Away from ℓ the extension is unramified, and v(κ) is divisible by m. Thus for Θ=Σ_{τ∈G}log_r(τ^(−1)ε mod λ)τ one obtains the **actual principal divisor**
div(κ)=mB−Θ λ
for an integral-coefficient fractional divisor B. The residue-log map is G-equivariant with this inverse indexing. Its reduction Θ mod p is in φ, and if ε is a nonresidue its identity coefficient is nonzero, hence Θ is invertible on φ. In the p-primary divisor quotient, m[B]=0, giving Θs[A]=0, contradicting [A]≠0. This proves the conditional one-prime contradiction without Artin reciprocity or Chebotarev.

**Sharper condition:** work with derivative modulus m=p and Q_φ=e_φ(A_p/pA_p), where A_p is the p-primary divisor quotient. Since [A]≠0, Q_φ≠0 (finite p-group Nakayama by iteration). For ℓ≡1(mod pq), a prime λ whose class has nonzero image in Q_φ and for which ε is a non-pth-power residue gives the same contradiction modulo p. This is the narrowest prime requirement **QP_φ**. Its existence, and an explicit bound, remain **OPEN**.

## 4. Which primes exist elementarily?
**PROVED — complete splitting:** For a monic integer polynomial f of degree d, choose a∈{0,…,d} with c=f(a)≠0, and let Q be twice the product of a prescribed finite bad-prime list (including polynomial discriminant). The polynomial g(T)=f(a+cQT)/c is integral and g(0)=1, g(j)≡1(mod Q). Among j=1,…,2d+1 some |g(j)|>1, since otherwise all would be roots of (g−1)(g+1), degree ≤2d. A prime ℓ dividing that value avoids Q, divides f(a+cQj), and satisfies ℓ≤(Σ|f_i|)(d+|c|Q(2d+1))^d/|c|. A root of f mod ℓ gives a degree-one factor, not generally complete splitting; complete splitting follows when Q(θ) is normal and ℓ avoids disc(f). For cyclotomic splitting use a prime divisor of Φ_N(t), where N=qm and t is a multiple of N and all excluded primes. It has exact order N mod ℓ, hence ℓ≡1(mod N), with ℓ≤(t+1)^φ(N). These bounds do **not** select a nontrivial Kummer action.

**PROVED — fixed-field selector:** For a finite Galois group Γ and subgroup H_0, the defining polynomial of its fixed field has a root at an unramified good ℓ exactly when Frobenius belongs to the union of conjugates of H_0. A selector allowing only identity and one nonidentity conjugacy class exists when that class has prime order and all nonidentity powers are conjugate. In a cyclotomic abelian group this forces order two. In the Kummer normal closure, conjugation by ζ_p↦ζ_p^u sends a relative t to t^u; thus a class of order p *does* admit an identity-or-detecting selector. Schur supplies primes in the union, not necessarily in its detecting branch. The commutator [σ_2,t]=t shows abelian congruence characters cannot distinguish those two branches.

**Murty–Thain criterion — source-audited, not imported as elementary:** the Euclidean-polynomial classification for rational progressions requires an occurrence clause: apart from finitely many exceptions, prime divisors of polynomial values lie in {1,a}, *and* primes in class a actually occur. The classification a²≡1(mod n) concerns this method. Its necessity and more general prime-occurrence statements in the read papers use Chebotarev. For order-two classes one can propagate primes by a finite polynomial/CRT construction given a good initial seed; e.g. 3 mod 8 is seed-free using prime divisors of Q²+2, which must be 1 or 3 mod 8 and whose product is 3 mod 8. No order-two mechanism gives the order-p circular detector.

**PROVED — source primes:** CASS gives x−1=p^(q−1)a^q, Φ_p(x)=pv^q, y+1=q^(p−1)b^p, Φ_q(−y)=qu^p. Outside p,q, primes dividing a or v give X≡0 and all source ratios A_r≡1; primes dividing b give X≡1 and A_r≡C_r/C_1; primes dividing u have ℓ≡1(mod q) and X of exact order q. The divided divisor A is supported over u. Since [A]≠0, at least one prime in this support has nonzero φ-component, with ℓ≤|u|. It need not represent exactly [A], lie in 1 mod p, or detect ε. For the *different* circular-split/source-detecting approach, primes dividing xy fail: at u-primes normalize the exceptional factor (X−η^a)/u^p≡X(X−1), and circular splitting plus the Cassels relation makes all normalized source-ratio p-symbols trivial. This exclusion does not apply to the circular-*nonresidue* Thaine prime.

## 5. Exact Frobenius requirements and stopping point
In the conditional classical encoding, an auxiliary prime for the strong m version must have identity Frobenius in B=Q(ζ_q,ζ_m), prescribed class s[A] under the Artin map in the Hilbert class field of k, and nonzero relative Frobenius in B(√[p]{τ ε}:τ∈G). The sharper Q_φ version requires identity in L=Q(ζ_pq), a nonzero Frobenius in the unramified quotient corresponding to Q_φ, and nonzero pairing with ε in its circular-unit Kummer extension. No prescribed Frobenius in L(√[p]{α}) is needed for this route. In the alternative C28 detector route one requires splitting in the circular-root field and nontrivial action on the source radical, a different condition.

The **finite** group, norm, derivative, residue, and valuation calculations above do not supply either (i) an elementary ideal-to-Frobenius correspondence or (ii) a prime realizing the simultaneous nontrivial class and circular Kummer restrictions. Thaine's Proposition 4 in the read 1987 preprint invokes Hilbert class fields, Artin and Chebotarev exactly here. A polynomial whose prime factors allow identity or the desired order-p action does not force the latter. No elementary substitute or bound for QP_φ is proved here.

**CONJECTURED / assessment:** a one-element elementary Thaine step is plausible as an algebraic calculation, but the simultaneous nonidentity-prime theorem remains a substantive obstacle. The even narrower direct target is PA_φ: produce β∈k* with div β=div(z)/p. Its solution bypasses auxiliary-prime existence and, by the proved unit-component result, closes the reflected source contradiction.
